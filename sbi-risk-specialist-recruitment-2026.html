SBI Risk Specialist eligibility

The mandatory qualification is CA, CFA, or an MBA/PGDM or equivalent degree in Finance, Data Analytics or Business Analytics from a recognised institute. Qualifications are assessed as on 31 October 2026.

At least two years of post-qualification work on Expected Credit Loss (ECL) related modelling is required in the notice's specified banking, financial, rating, brokerage or consulting-risk organisations. Teaching, training and internship experience do not count. Preferred FRM/PRM certification is not a replacement for mandatory qualification and experience.

Age, vacancies and posting

The advertisement lists three unreserved Risk Specialist vacancies at MMGS-II, including the stated horizontal disability provision. Age is 25 to 35 years as on 31 October 2026, with relaxation only where applicable under the notice.

SBI decides posting, transfer and use of selected officers. Candidates may be posted across India; this is not an Amb, Una or Himachal-specific posting offer.

SBI Risk Specialist apply online

The SBI Risk Specialist Recruitment 2026 last date for online registration and payment is 23 November 2026. Start from the bank's official Current Openings page and choose advertisement CRPD/SCO/2026-27/27, not another SBI specialist recruitment.

Prepare clear qualification, age and experience documents, CV/biodata and the other applicable certificates listed by the bank. Review the submitted record and retain the acknowledgement. Do not send a hard-copy application to this assistance centre as if it were SBI.

Experience documents matter

A job title alone does not prove ECL modelling experience. Match the work described in your employer records to the notice. Eligibility, shortlisting and interview decisions belong to SBI. Jan Seva Point can help with document scanning and form preparation but cannot confirm selection.
