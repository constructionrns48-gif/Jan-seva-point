ISRO URSC Driver Syllabus and Exam Pattern 2026

Looking for the ISRO driver syllabus 2026? This page explains the ISRO driver exam pattern for URSC:04:2026, including the light vehicle driver syllabus and the Heavy Vehicle Driver-A and Staff Car Driver-A subjects. It is not a Scientist, Technician or another ISRO centre’s syllabus.

Official driver syllabus and exam pattern

This is the driver syllabus in URSC:04:2026, not the Technician-B trade syllabus.

Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, as amended50 questions
Elementary English, Class 8 level15 questions
Elementary arithmetic, Class 10 level15 questions
General knowledge20 questions

The written test has 100 multiple-choice questions in 120 minutes. Each correct answer earns 1 mark; each wrong answer loses 0.25 marks.

Motor Vehicles Act topics include driver licensing, vehicle registration, transport vehicles, traffic control, insurance, offences and penalties, mandatory signs, accident claims and Claims Tribunals.

English covers synonyms, antonyms, correct verbs and filling blanks. Arithmetic covers addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, percentages, ratios and averages. General knowledge includes current affairs, who is who, Indian states and capitals, and Indian geography.

The driving skill test is qualifying: 60 out of 100 marks are needed. The notice sets separate written-test qualifying rules and category conditions. Read those rules before using a score to judge eligibility.
