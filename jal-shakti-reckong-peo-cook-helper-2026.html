Posts and honorarium

1 Para Cook + 1 Para Helper

Para Cook: ₹9,000/month · Para Helper: ₹5,500/month

Work location: Reckong Peo Division, Kinnaur district. This is not a statewide permanent recruitment.

Eligibility to check

  • Para Cook: Matriculation plus a degree/diploma from a government-approved Hotel Management, Institute of Hotel Management or Food Production institute, or at least 5 years of cooking/catering experience in a government or private institution.
  • Para Helper: Matriculation from a board/university recognised by the HP or Central Government.
  • Both posts: age 18–45, Indian citizen and bonafide Himachali. Permanent residence within the area under Jal Shakti Circle Reckong Peo is required. The notice also requires that no family member is in a Government/PSU/Board job, medical fitness and no involvement in criminal activity.
  • Read the school-location condition and its bonafide-Himachali exception in the original notice.

How to apply

Use the prescribed proforma in the official notice. The completed application and required supporting documents had to reach the Executive Engineer, Jal Shakti Division Reckong Peo, District Kinnaur, Himachal Pradesh, by 7 November 2026, 5:00 PM IST. This is the office receipt deadline, not the posting date. Late or incomplete applications can be rejected.

Check the original notice for the full document list, certificate validity, experience rules and the application proforma. Keep copies of education, age, bonafide Himachali and applicable category/experience records ready.

Selection and conditions

Merit assessment and the prescribed skill/physical tests apply as stated for each post. Read the official criteria and subsequent test notices; there is no selection guarantee.
