Job application assistance at Jan Seva Point, Lok Mitra Kendra Amb

Candidates from Amb and Una district looking for government job form-filling help can contact Jan Seva Point, Lok Mitra Kendra in Amb, for online application assistance, document scanning and photo/signature preparation or upload, subject to the recruiting portal requirements.

Check the official notice, eligibility and last date first. Assistance depends on an application window being open; a listing or old notice is not proof that applications are open. Jan Seva Point is an independent assistance centre, not a government department or recruiting authority. Eligibility and selection remain with the recruiting authority.
