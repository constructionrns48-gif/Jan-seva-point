Essential qualifications and HP-schooling condition

  • 10+2 Science from a recognised board/university, plus a recognised degree from the advertisement’s list: B.Sc. Medical Technology in Radiology & Imaging, Radio Diagnosis & Radiotherapy, X-Ray/Radio Diagnosis, Radiation Technology, Medical Imaging Technology, Radio Diagnosis & Imaging, Radiography & Imaging; or listed Allied Health Science/Medical Imaging, Medical Radio & Imaging, Bachelor of Radiation & Imaging Technology, Bachelor of Radiation Technology (Lateral Entry) routes.
