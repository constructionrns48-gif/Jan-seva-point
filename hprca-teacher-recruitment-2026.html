Read the appointment terms first

  • Scheme-based engagement in CBSE-affiliated schools for five years. Purely temporary, not against sanctioned posts; no right to regularisation. Honorarium is ₹30,000 per month for ten months in an academic year, not twelve months.
  • 2,423 is the sum of the fifteen subject totals printed in the official advertisement, not a single printed grand-total line. Post codes run from 26046 to 26060. Category break-ups must be checked in the original tables.

English and Mathematics are not a generic eligibility rule

  • English/Mathematics: relevant M.A./M.Sc.; graduation and B.Ed. minimum 50%, plus at least 55% in the relevant subject at graduation. The subject must be compulsory in B.Ed.; listed integrated B.Ed. routes are eligible. Pass the prescribed HP TET.
  • For SC/ST/OBC candidates the English/Mathematics note specifies 45% in graduation/B.Ed. and 50% in the graduation subject. Do not apply one relaxation rule to every subject.

Other subject qualifications

  • Hindi, Political Science, History and Sanskrit: relevant Master’s 50%, B.Ed.50%, prescribed TET, proficiency in teaching Hindi and English. The ad allows up to 5% educational-marks relaxation for the stated HP SC/ST/OBC/PWD categories.
  • Economics accepts Economics/Applied Economics/Business Economics; Physics accepts Physics/Electronics/Nuclear Physics; Chemistry accepts Chemistry/Biochemistry. Relevant Master’s 50%, B.Ed.50%, TET and language-proficiency conditions apply.
  • Biology: Master’s 50% in the listed biological fields, with Botany and Zoology studied at graduation; B.Ed.50%, TET and language proficiency. Music: Master’s in Vocal/Instrumental 50%, B.Ed.50%, TET and language proficiency.
  • Commerce: Master’s 50% in Accountancy/Financial Accountancy and Income Tax/MFC (Master of Finance Control); B.Ed.50%, TET and language proficiency. Fine Arts: Master’s Fine/Visual Arts (Painting and Sculpture)50%, OR Higher Secondary/Intermediate/Senior School plus recognised 5-year full-time/7-year part-time Fine Art/Painting diploma 50%. Read the exact recognition and relaxation conditions.

Computer: five alternative routes

  • B.E./B.Tech CS/IT; OR B.E./B.Tech any stream + PG Diploma in Computer Science/Technology/Applications; OR M.Sc. Computer Science/MCA; OR PG Mathematics/Physics + PG Diploma in Computer Science/Applications; OR DOEACC “B”/“C” level. Minimum 50% aggregate as specified for each route.
  • Proficiency in teaching Hindi and English. Up to 5% educational-marks relaxation for the stated HP SC/ST/OBC/PWD categories. Do not assume B.Ed./TET is required here just because another subject requires it.

Physical Education: check the NCTE route

  • The ad lists NCTE 2009 routes involving B.P.Ed./BPE/B.Sc. Health and Physical Education and Degree in Sports at 55%; OR NCTE 2007 B.P.Ed./integrated 4-year professional degree at 50%; OR the NCTE 2002 B.P.Ed./3-year B.P.E. route with its stated 55%/50% requirements.
  • Another route lists one-year D.P.Ed. under NCTE norms AND M.P.Ed./M.A. Physical Education of at least 2 years from an NCTE-recognised institution. Because the original wording is route-specific, match your certificate to page 15 of the ad; a 10+2-plus-diploma shortcut is not an accurate summary.

Age, selection and application

  • Age 18–45 as on 1 January 2026. Applications open 10 November 2026 at 10:00 AM and close 5 December 2026 at 11:59 PM IST. Use HPRCA OTR/online application; no offline applications.
  • The ad describes a 120-mark,90-minute CBT/written screening test, or as decided by the Aayog. General qualifying minimum 45%; specified SC/ST/OBC/WFF categories 40%, with conditional further relaxation where the ad permits. Check later examination notices and document-verification instructions.

HP TET and M.A./B.Ed. marks requirements

There is no single qualification rule for all 15 subjects. English and Mathematics have separate graduation-subject marks and B.Ed. conditions; see their section above. For subjects such as Political Science, the advertisement requires the relevant Master’s degree and B.Ed. with the stated marks, plus the prescribed TET.

Where TET is prescribed, the notice names H.P. Board of School Education, Dharamshala, or another government-authorised agency. Computer, Physical Education and Fine Arts have their own routes; do not add a TET or B.Ed. condition that their entry does not state. Check the subject entry in pages 11–16 of Advt 14/2026.

Political Science Teacher eligibility

Post code 26049: Master’s degree in the subject concerned from a recognised university with at least 50% aggregate, B.Ed. from a recognised university/institution with at least 50%, and the prescribed TET. Proficiency in teaching Hindi and English is required.

The note allows up to 5% relaxation in minimum educational qualifications for the stated SC/ST/OBC/PWD categories of Himachal Pradesh. Read the exact conditions on page 12 of the advertisement; a degree alone does not confirm eligibility.

Age limit, reservation and HP schooling

  • Age is 18–45 as on 1 January 2026. Match any claimed relaxation to the original notice and applicable category rules.
  • Reservation benefits are state-specific and apply to bona fide Himachal Pradesh residents under the stated rules. Reserved-category candidates from other states are treated as General/Unreserved.
  • The notice requires Matriculation and 10+2 from a school/institution in Himachal Pradesh, but expressly exempts bona fide Himachalis from this schooling condition.
  • Category certificates must meet the notice’s validity conditions at the application closing date and document verification. Check the category-wise vacancy tables before choosing a post.

Online application process

  1. Open the official HPRCA website and read Advertisement 14/2026 and any amendments.
  2. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) before filling the Online Recruitment Application (ORA).
  3. When the window starts on 10 November 2026 at 10:00 AM IST, select the correct subject/post code and enter details matching the Matric certificate.
  4. Upload the prescribed photograph and signature, complete all mandatory portal steps, and submit before 5 December 2026 at 11:59 PM IST. Follow the official portal’s payment instructions.
  5. Keep the downloaded application for document verification. Only one application per post is permitted; offline applications are not accepted.

Required documents and upload preparation

  • Use your Matric certificate for name, parents’ names and date of birth. Keep the qualifications required for your chosen subject, applicable B.Ed./TET records and category certificates ready for verification.
  • Upload a latest passport-size photograph not older than one month and a scanned signature. Page 16 specifies JPG/JPEG/PNG, maximum 1 MB for each.
  • Bring the downloaded application, necessary original certificates and self-attested photocopies to document verification, or provide them when HPRCA asks. This is a preparation summary, not a substitute for the post/category-specific checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Are these permanent government teacher posts?

No. The notice describes a five-year, temporary scheme-based engagement, not appointment against sanctioned posts, with no right to regularisation. Honorarium is ₹30,000 per month for ten months in an academic year.

When can I apply?

The published window is 10 November 2026, 10:00 AM, to 5 December 2026, 11:59 PM IST.

Do all subjects have the same TET and marks rules?

No. Match your qualification to the subject’s entry. English/Mathematics, Computer, Physical Education and Fine Arts have distinct conditions.

Can I apply offline?

No. Register on HPRCA OTR and use the official online application route. Read amendments and portal instructions before submission.
