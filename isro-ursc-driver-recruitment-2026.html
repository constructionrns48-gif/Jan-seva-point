Vacancies and posting

  • Light Vehicle Driver-A: 3 (UR 2, SC 1). Staff Car Driver-A: 2 (UR 2), identified for Department of Space. Heavy Vehicle Driver-A: 10 (UR 6, OBC 2, SC 1, EWS 1), including 2 UR posts identified for MCF Hassan.
  • Ex-servicemen reservation overlaps these totals: URSC has 2 LVD and 6 HVD vacancies reserved for ex-servicemen; MCF has 1 HVD vacancy. It is not an extra set of posts.

Education, licence and experience

  • All three driver posts require SSLC/SSC/Matriculation or equivalent. LVD and Staff Car Driver require 3 years of light-vehicle driving experience and a valid light-vehicle driving licence.
  • HVD requires 5 years of driving experience, including at least 3 years driving heavy vehicles and the balance driving light motor vehicles, plus a valid HVD licence and Public Service Badge where statutory. Read the Karnataka joining-condition exceptions in the notice.
  • Experience must be after obtaining the valid licence. Part-time experience is excluded. Certificates must come from government/semi-government agencies or registered companies/societies/trusts, not individuals.

Dates, age and selection

  • Applications run from 24 October 2026, 5:00 PM to 13 November 2026, 11:55 PM IST. Age, qualifications and experience are assessed as on the closing date. Category relaxation applies only under the notice conditions; read vacancy-specific rules.
  • Selection includes a written/computer-based test and a driving skill test. All driver posts are Level 2, Rs 19,900 per month plus admissible allowances. Rs 21,700 in this advertisement is for Technician-B, not drivers.
