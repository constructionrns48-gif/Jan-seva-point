DSSSB MTS Syllabus and Exam Pattern 2026 | 810/26

This DSSSB MTS syllabus 2026 guide explains the DSSSB MTS exam pattern 2026 for post code 810/26. For the DSSSB MTS syllabus in Hindi, use the Hindi button above. The Junior Engineer scheme is separate.

Official MTS syllabus and exam pattern

Use the One Tier (General) scheme for MTS-family post code 810/26. The Junior Engineer technical scheme for 809/26 is different.

General awareness40 marks
General intelligence and reasoning ability40 marks
Arithmetical and numerical ability40 marks
Hindi language and comprehension40 marks
English language and comprehension40 marks

The test has 200 multiple-choice questions for 200 marks, with a duration of 2 hours. Each question carries 1 mark. A wrong answer loses 0.25 marks.

Questions are bilingual in Hindi and English, except language-paper questions, which are in the language concerned.

The final merit list is based on examination marks, subject to the minimum qualifying criteria. DSSSB may change the scheme before the examination. This section summarises the official subject headings, not a separate coaching syllabus.
