CRPF Sports Quota Recruitment 2026

Only eligible meritorious sportspersons · 521 posts

The official notice gives an application window from 12 October to 11 November 2026, 11:59 PM IST. Eligible Indian men and women, including Himachal candidates, must meet all sports, education, age and physical conditions.

Posts and education

Head Constable/GD: 182 posts, 12th or equivalent. Constable/GD: 339 posts, 10th or equivalent. Total 521 tentative vacancies, subject to change. Required certificates must be held by the closing date.

Sports eligibility is mandatory

Passing 10th/12th alone is not enough. The notice requires specified recognised national/international, university, school or Khelo India participation or achievements, depending on the post and sport, with certificates from the prescribed authority. Sports achievements/certificates must fall within the preceding two years from advertisement publication. Read the original post-wise sports rules and discipline list.

Age and selection

Base age is 18–23 as on 1 August 2026. The notice separately provides sportsperson and category relaxations; do not treat the base range as the final upper-age limit without those rules. Documentation, physical standards, sports trials and medical conditions apply.
