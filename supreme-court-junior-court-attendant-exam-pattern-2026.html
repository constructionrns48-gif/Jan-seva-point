Supreme Court Junior Court Attendant 2026: Exam Pattern and Subjects

Searching for the Supreme Court Junior Court Attendant syllabus 2026? The official notice supplies subject groups and an exam pattern, not a detailed topic-wise syllabus. This page covers Attendant, not Junior Court Assistant or a cooking-knowledge examination.

Official exam pattern and subjects

The notice gives subject groups and the examination scheme, not a detailed topic-by-topic syllabus.

General intelligence and reasoning25 questions
Numerical aptitude25 questions
General English25 questions
General awareness25 questions

The objective test has 100 questions for 100 marks in 90 minutes. The minimum qualifying mark is 50. Each question carries 1 mark, with 0.25 marks deducted for an incorrect answer.

The interview carries 25 marks, with 13 as the minimum qualifying mark. Admission to the test and interview is provisional; document checks and the notice conditions still apply.

The application-link notice states that the objective test will be in English only. This is the ordinary Junior Court Attendant recruitment in this notice, not a cooking-knowledge examination.
