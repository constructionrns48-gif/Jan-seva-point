HPPSC ADO Syllabus and Exam Pattern 2026

This HPPSC ADO syllabus 2026 guide covers the official exam pattern for Agriculture Development Officer in Himachal Pradesh, advertisement 63/9-2026. Detailed SAT topics still need matching to this recruitment; they are not presented as verified here.

Official exam pattern: Paper-I and Paper-II

General knowledge of Himachal Pradesh30 marks
National and international general knowledge30 marks
Hindi language20 marks
English language20 marks

Paper-I is a one-hour CBT or offline screening test for 100 marks. Incorrect answers attract negative marking. Read the notice rules for option E and unanswered questions as well; no deduction rate is invented here.

Paper-II is a three-hour descriptive Subject Aptitude Test (SAT) for 120 marks. A personality test follows under the advertisement conditions.
