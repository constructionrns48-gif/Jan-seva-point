Do not decide eligibility from the post title alone

Read the post-wise educational qualifications in Annexure A of the official CEN, including the required marks, applicable exceptions and any typing requirement. This page does not confirm an individual candidate's eligibility. Candidates awaiting the final result of the prescribed qualification should not apply under the original notice.

Match the post's medical standard and reservation conditions. Read the RRB-wise and Railway/Production Unit-wise vacancy details in Annexure B. These are national Railway vacancies, not a promise of a posting in Himachal Pradesh.

The original CEN permits selection of only one RRB. Multiple applications against this CEN can lead to rejection and debarment. Use your own working mobile number and email and check every entry before submission.

Application dates and route

The original CEN opens applications on 15 October 2026 and closes submission on 13 November 2026 at 11:59 PM IST. The fee-payment date for submitted applications is 15 November 2026. It is not an extension of the application-submission deadline.

The published modification window is 16-25 November 2026, with a non-refundable Rs 250 modification fee for each occasion. Details entered when creating the account and the chosen RRB cannot be changed under the notice.

Use the application link provided on the official RRB website. Check the full examination-fee table and refund conditions before payment. No CBT or document-verification date is promised on this page; the notice says these will be communicated through official RRB pages.
