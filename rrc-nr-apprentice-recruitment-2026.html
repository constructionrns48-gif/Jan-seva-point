RRC NR Apprentice Recruitment 2026

Northern Railway Apprentice 2026 applications cover 4,651 Act Apprentice training slots. This is training under the Apprentices Act, not a promise of a permanent railway job.

Before applying

Training is not permanent employment. Educational qualification cut-off: 6 Nov; age cut-off: 11 Dec. Do not confuse these dates.

Application dates and eligibility cut-offs

The RRC NR Apprentice Recruitment 2026 last date is 11 December 2026 at 23:59 IST. The online window opened on 11 November. The educational qualification must already have been passed on the notification date, 6 November 2026; results awaited on that date do not qualify.

10th and ITI requirements

You need SSC/Matriculation/10th or equivalent with at least 50% aggregate, plus the relevant ITI trade certificate from an NCVT/SCVT-recognised route. Check your exact trade against the cluster and unit table before choosing. An application to a unit without slots for your trade can be rejected.

Applicants must have completed 15 years and must not have completed 24 years on 11 December 2026. SC/ST, OBC, disability and ex-servicemen relaxations follow the original notification.

What the training does and does not offer

The 4,651 slots are spread across Northern Railway divisions, workshops and units, including the Ambala cluster. This is not an Amb or Una posting announcement. Training does not confer a right to railway employment, and the employer is not obliged to offer a job after training.

Selection uses the notice's merit and document/medical rules, not a promise from this assistance centre. The notice says there is no written test or viva for this engagement.
