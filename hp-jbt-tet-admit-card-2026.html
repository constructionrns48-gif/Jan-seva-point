HP JBT TET Admit Card 2026

The HP JBT TET Admit Card 2026 is for the November teacher eligibility test. HPBOSE schedules JBT TET for Sunday, 22 November 2026, from 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM. This guide explains the expected admit-card timing, the official download route and how to ask Jan Seva Point for download or print help.

November exam date and eligibility-test status

The HP JBT TET November 2026 exam date is 22 November, afternoon shift. Other TET subjects have different dates and shifts. Follow the date, centre and reporting instructions on your own card. TET is an eligibility test, not a job vacancy announcement or a guarantee of appointment.

Official website and release-date checks

The HP JBT TET admit card 2026 official website is hpbose.org, operated by HPBOSE, not Jan Seva Point. A current Board announcement should confirm the HP TET admit card 2026 release date; an expected date is not a release notice.

Start at the Board's website and select the November 2026 TET service. A generic admit-card page or a link existing on the site does not by itself confirm that your November card is ready.

Download and login help

The HP JBT TET admit card download process should be completed through HPBOSE after release. The prospectus specifies the application number and date of birth for access. Use the Board's November TET page to reach the HP TET admit card 2026 login. Check the address before entering personal details.

  1. Keep your application number and date of birth ready.
  2. Open the official November TET service and follow the admit-card option when available.
  3. Check your name, photograph, subject, examination date, shift and allotted centre.
  4. Save and print the card. Read its instructions before travelling.

If the link or record is unavailable, check current Board notices and contact HPBOSE for clarification. Do not edit an admit-card PDF or assume that a local printout can correct a Board record.

What to take and what to check

The prospectus lists the admit card, Aadhaar or another photo ID, a simple transparent blue/black ballpoint pen and an additional photograph for the attendance sheet. Read your card for the final instructions and any later Board change. You must attend the allotted examination centre in person; remote download assistance does not replace attendance.

Download or print assistance in Amb

Jan Seva Point, Lok Mitra Kendra Amb, can help with downloading or printing after HPBOSE releases the card. HPBOSE alone issues the admit card. This is an independent information page, not a government portal. Do not post your application details in a public message.
