HPSCB General Manager Recruitment 2026

HPSCB General Manager Recruitment 2026 concerns one senior-officer post at the Himachal Pradesh State Co-operative Bank Ltd., Head Office, The Mall, Shimla. The criteria refer to the advertisement dated 13 November 2026. Applications must reach through the proper channel by 15 December 2026.

Official advertisement and criteria checked: 17 November 2026 · Jan Seva Point is not the recruiting bank
Not a fresher or general graduate recruitment

This opportunity is only for eligible serving officers of NABARD or nationalised banks. A degree and ten years of general banking experience alone are not enough. Employer consent/recommendation is compulsory.

Who is eligible?

The HPSCB recruitment 2026 qualification for this post is graduation, together with all the service conditions below:

  • At least ten years of work experience as an officer with NABARD or a nationalised bank.
  • Not below Assistant General Manager rank, with at least three years as Assistant General Manager in the parent organisation.
  • Age 40 years and above, but not exceeding 56 years. The notice does not state an age cut-off date.

A professional banking qualification such as CAIIB, Diploma in Banking and Finance, Diploma in Co-operative Business Management, or a postgraduate/equivalent qualification is preferred.

Deputation term and pay

The HPSCB General Manager deputation is initially for one year and may be extended on a need basis. The criteria state Level 21 of the Punjab State Government Pay Matrix, starting basic pay ₹67,400, plus admissible DA stated as 50% of basic pay in the notice.

Listed allowances are HRA ₹2,900, compensatory allowance ₹250, capital allowance ₹400 and mobile allowance ₹400. ₹67,400 is basic pay, not take-home salary. The listed leave entitlements are 33 earned, 15 casual and 20 medical leave.

How to apply through your employer

The HPSCB recruitment 2026 last date for receipt is 15 December 2026. No closing time is printed. Start the employer-forwarding process early.

  1. Read both the official advertisement and general criteria linked below.
  2. Prepare an application on plain paper with complete bio-data, present salary/allowances, expected salary and applicable deputation terms.
  3. Obtain the employer/HOD's consent or recommendation, an employer-issued integrity certificate, and a statement regarding major disciplinary penalties during the last ten years.
  4. Submit through the proper channel to the Managing Director, HPSCB. The criteria specify email at recruitment@hpscb.bank.in.

Direct applications without employer consent/recommendation will not be entertained. These documents do not provide an online application form, specify an application fee amount or give an exam/interview schedule. Do not assume another HPSCB recruitment's online form applies.
