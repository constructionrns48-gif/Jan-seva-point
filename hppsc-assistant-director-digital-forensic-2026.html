Role, qualification and experience

  • 1 unreserved post in the Directorate of Forensic Services, Department of Home, HP. Engagement is under the Job Trainee scheme dated 19 July 2025, not an offer of regular permanent appointment.
  • At least second-class M.Sc. in Forensic Science, Digital Forensics, Cyber Forensics or Physics; or MCA; or a Master Degree in Engineering in Computer Science, Electronics, Information and Technology (IT) or Electronic Communication, from a recognised university.
  • Minimum 7 years of post-qualification research, training and analytical experience in the specified subjects. The certificate must be signed by the Research Supervisor or Head of the Organisation/Department.
  • The 21 October corrigendum corrects the Ph.D.-research clause: Ph.D. research counts toward the required seven years, not three years.

Age and selection

  • Age 18–45 as on 1 January 2026. Category-based age concessions depend on the advertisement and reserved-post conditions; this is an unreserved vacancy. Eligibility and experience are assessed at the ORA closing date.
  • Follow the Commission’s screening/subject aptitude test and personality-test procedure in the advertisement. Exam dates and admission remain subject to official notices; submission is not selection.

Application checklist

  • Use HPPSC’s official website → Apply Online → One Time Registration. Create or use your own profile, choose the correct advertisement and upload readable certificates before final submission.
  • Prepare Matric age proof, degrees and all-year marksheets, relevant experience certificates and applicable category/bonafide records. Ph.D. and HP customs/dialects knowledge are listed as desirable, not substitutes for essential qualifications.
