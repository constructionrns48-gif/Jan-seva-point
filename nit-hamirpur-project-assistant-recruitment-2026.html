Project, appointment and monthly amount

The project is "Strategic Cost Control and Quality Enhancement in Construction Projects using Industry 4.0 Technologies", funded under the stated CSR programme. The notice lists one Project Assistant, Rs 22,000 per month including Rs 20,000 plus 10% HRA (Rs 2,000).

The initial appointment is for one year from joining, extendable up to the project duration subject to satisfactory performance. The stated project duration is 1.5 years. This is not a regular permanent NIT post.
