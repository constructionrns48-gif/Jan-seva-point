Post and eligibility

  • 3 clear-cut vacancies: 2 UR and 1 EWS. Graduation plus 3 years as a Steno-typist, Judgment Writer or Stenographer in a government office or reputed organisation.
  • English shorthand 100 WPM; transcription within 5 times the dictation time. English computer typing 50 WPM. More than 10% mistakes in transcription or typing disqualifies the candidate.
  • Age 18–45 for UR/EWS as on 1 January 2026; HP government employees 18–50 under the stated rules. Other relaxations are conditional: read the category and reserved-post provisions. Qualifications and experience are assessed on application submission.

Selection and important conditions

  • Competitive examination under Schedule IV, Part H of the High Court R&P Rules, including the prescribed shorthand/typing tests. Check the official examination scheme and later notices for the schedule.
  • Appointment is subject to the outcome of CWP 6948/2025 and CWP 7805/2025. Vacancy numbers can change. This page does not guarantee selection.

Prepare before applying

  • Keep graduation records, Matric age proof, identity proof, experience certificate and applicable category records ready. In-service candidates must follow the NOC/proper-channel rules.
  • Use the High Court-designated recruitment portal. The published window required the online form and payment before 7 November 2026, 11:59 PM IST. Other application modes are not accepted.
