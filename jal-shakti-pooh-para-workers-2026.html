Posts and honorarium

2 Para Pump Operators · 1 Para Fitter · 17 Multi Purpose Workers

Pump Operator / Fitter: ₹7,100/month · MPW: ₹6,000/month

Work location: Pooh Division, Kinnaur district. This is not a statewide permanent recruitment.

Eligibility to check

  • Multi Purpose Worker: Middle (8th) pass or equivalent from a school recognised by the state Government; the 8th-standard marks sheet is required.
  • Para Pump Operator: Matriculation (10th) plus the specified relevant ITI certificate, or the recognised skill-development route stated in the notice. The notice lists eligible electrical, mechanical and pump-operation trades; check your exact certificate against it.
  • Para Fitter: Matriculation (10th) plus ITI in Fitter/Plumber, or the skill-development certificate route specified in the notice.
  • Age 18–45 as on 13 November 2026. Bonafide residence certificate and applicable category/document rules must be met.

How to apply

Use the prescribed proforma in the official notice. The completed application and required supporting documents must reach the Executive Engineer, Jal Shakti Division Pooh, District Kinnaur, Himachal Pradesh, by 13 November 2026, 5:00 PM IST. This is the office receipt deadline, not the posting date. Late or incomplete applications can be rejected.

Check the original notice for the full document list, certificate validity, experience rules and the application proforma. Keep copies of education, age, bonafide Himachali and applicable category/experience records ready.
