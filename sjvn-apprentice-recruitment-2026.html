SJVN Apprentice Recruitment 2026

SJVN Limited Advt. No. 128/2026 offers 100 one-year apprenticeship training seats for Himachal Pradesh domicile candidates: 40 Graduate, 25 Diploma and 35 ITI. This is training under the Apprentices Act, not a permanent SJVN job.

Before applying

Online registration opens 18 November 2026 on the SJVN website; the detailed advertisement appears on the SJVN career page. Training is not permanent employment, and only Himachal Pradesh domicile candidates are eligible.

Application dates

Online registration for the SJVN Apprentice Recruitment 2026 runs from 18 November 2026 to 17 December 2026 through the Online Recruitment Portal on the SJVN website (career section). The short notification (Advt. No. 128/2026) was published in November 2026; read the detailed advertisement on the SJVN career page before applying. The SJVN recruitment 2026 notification PDF is hosted only on the official SJVN website; download it from sjvn.nic.in itself and be careful of lookalike copies on other sites.

Seats and qualifications

SJVN ITI, Diploma and Graduate apprentice 2026 seats are divided by branch and trade as follows.

Graduate Apprentices · 40 seatsCivil 15 · Electrical 10 · Mechanical 10 · Information Technology 5Full-time 4-year Bachelor degree in Engineering in the relevant branch from an AICTE-recognised institute/university
Technician (Diploma) Apprentices · 25 seatsCivil 10 · Electrical 5 · Mechanical 5 · Information Technology 5Full-time 3-year polytechnic diploma in Engineering in the relevant branch
Technician (ITI) Apprentices · 35 seatsElectrician 15 · Stenographer & Secretarial Assistant 5 · Fitter 5 · Welder 5 · Mechanic (Electronics/Instrument) 5Matriculation plus a regular full-time ITI course in the respective trade recognised by NCVT/SCVT

Age limit and eligibility

Minimum 18 years and maximum 30 years as on the closing date (17 December 2026). Upper-age relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL) and up to 10 years for PwBD UR/EWS, 15 years for PwBD SC/ST and 13 years for PwBD OBC (NCL) candidates, as per the notification.

This SJVN vacancy 2026 is open to Himachal Pradesh domicile candidates only; applications from other states are not accepted. Apply for only one trade or discipline; where multiple applications are submitted, the latest one is treated as final. Candidates who have already undergone apprenticeship training in SJVN, and result-awaited candidates, are not eligible.

Stipend and what the training offers

Monthly stipend: Graduate Apprentices ₹15,000, Technician (Diploma) Apprentices ₹13,500, Technician (ITI) Apprentices ₹12,500. Training duration is one year under the Apprenticeship Act, 1961.

This apprenticeship does not confer any right to employment in SJVN, and SJVN is not obliged to offer a job after the training year.

How to apply online

For SJVN Apprentice 2026 apply online only through the official SJVN career page: find Advt. No. 128/2026 and use the Apply Online link. Register with your name, e-mail and password, fill the form in steps with Save and Next, and review names, marks and certificate details before final submission. Keep the final acknowledgement.

Common questions about SJVN Recruitment 2026

What is the SJVN Apprenticeship 2026 last date?

Online registration opens 18 November 2026 and the last date to apply is 17 December 2026. Apply well before the closing date; apprentice deadlines are rarely extended.

What is the SJVN apprentice salary (stipend) in 2026?

It is a monthly stipend, not a salary: ₹15,000 for Graduate Apprentices, ₹13,500 for Technician (Diploma) and ₹12,500 for Technician (ITI) Apprentices.

What are the SJVN recruitment 2026 eligibility and qualification rules?

You need a Himachal Pradesh domicile, age 18 to 30 on the closing date (relaxation for SC/ST, OBC and PwBD), and a full-time BE/B.Tech, polytechnic diploma or ITI in the relevant branch or trade, as listed above.

Is there an SJVN exam date 2026 for these apprentice seats?

The notice does not mention a written exam; SJVN shortlists apprentice candidates on merit. If SJVN announces any test or document-verification date, it will appear on the official career page.

Verified fee and selection details

Application fee: Rs 100; SC, ST, EWS and PwBD applicants are exempt. The notice specifies no interview and merit-based shortlisting: Graduate 20% Class 10 + 20% Class 12/Diploma (higher if both) + 60% Engineering; Diploma 30% Class 10 + 70% Diploma; ITI 30% Class 10 + 70% ITI. Photo and signature files must be JPG/JPEG, less than 500 KB. Read the official notice for all conditions.
